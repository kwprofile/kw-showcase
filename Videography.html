Short Films

BUDDY (2026)

Contributing Roles: Video/Sound Editor, Colourist, Actor
Programs I Worked With: Adobe Premiere Pro

Description: In Carleton University's "Film and Media Practice II" course, we got the opportunity to create short films within small production teams. My role within our group was primarily to bring together the director's (Tristan Deschamps') vision, tying together our production elements (Kat Farrell as the cinematographer, and Zixi Ning as the sound designer/composer) into a fully edited 20 minute film. I was also responsible for overseeing the colour grading and pacing for the film, with my teammates giving me creative input for each draft. This experience taught our team how to self-manage and execute a creative project with a professionally collaborative work ethic. It was an incredible pleasure to have worked with my group, as we had maintained a highly organized production schedule and consistent communication throughout the whole process. Our project started its scriptwriting and storyboarding phase in February of 2026, and completed production in early April of 2026.

Two's a Pittance (2025)

Contributing Roles: Director, Cinematographer, Video/Sound Editor, Colourist, Sound Production, Scriptwriter, Locations Scout
Programs I Worked With: DaVinci Resolve, Audacity

Description: During my first practical filmmaking course at Carleton University ("Film and Media Practice I"), I had to create an original short film to demonstrate my capabilities as a solo filmmaker. I was responsible for the full creative execution of this project, managing the production scheduling and casting for my crew. During this filmmaking experience, I learned the importance of pre-planning the staging and composition of all my shots, allowing for a smoother work ethic during filming days. Making this project was very enjoyable, with my friends volunteering as actors and making days on set highly fun (with some inside jokes even making it into the film). Two's a Pittance started its scriptwritting stage in September of 2025, and completed production in December of 2025.

Open Worlds (2022)

Contributing Roles: Director, Scriptwriter, Cinematographer, Video/Sound Editor, Colourist, Sound Production, Animator
Programs I Worked With: Adobe Premiere Pro, Krita

Description: In my final year of high school, I took a media production class where I had to create an original short film. This film was an important one, serving as many stepping stones in my early filmmaking journey. I even personally challenged myself to try superimposing a 2D animated character into live-action footage (and successfully learning how to chroma key my animated character). This was my first short film above 3 minutes, which I was very proud of at the time. I love creating mixed-media art very much, and aim to do more things like this for personal projects in the future.

Commercial Videos

Inspect This Car - Lighthouse Films Capstone Project (2024)

Contributing Roles: Team Second, Creative Consultant, Client Communications, Managing Project Deliverables
Programs I Worked With: Microsoft Suite and Basecamp

Description: During my final term at Algonquin College's Interactive Media Design program, I collaborated with a film production team (6 members) for a final capstone project. I was elected as a Team Second, working alongside my Team Lead (Karlin Villasis) to manage the project's deliverables, creative direction, scope, and professional client-to-team communication. Our goal was to help promote Ali Yaqub's independent business by creating a well-polished commercial video (Edited by James Lazaro), as well as a social media marketing strategy for various platforms. Our project proved successful, with our client satisfied with the final outcome. This project spanned across three intensive months, from May 2024 to August 2024.
