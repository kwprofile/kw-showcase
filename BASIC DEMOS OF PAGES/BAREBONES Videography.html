Short Films

BUDDY (2026)

Contributing Roles: Editor, Colour Grader, Actor
Programs I Worked With: Adobe Premiere Pro

Description: In Carleton University's "Film and Media Practice II" course, we got the opportunity to create short films within small production teams. My role within our group was primarily to bring together the director's (Tristan Deschamps') vision, tying together our production elements (Kat Farrell as the cinematographer, and Zixi Ning as the sound designer/composer) into a fully edited 20 minute film. I was also responsible for overseeing the colour grading and pacing for the film, with my teammates giving me creative input for each draft. This experience taught our team how to self-manage and execute a creative project with a professionally collaborative work ethic. It was an incredible pleasure to have worked with my group, as we had maintained a highly organized production schedule and consistent communication throughout the whole process. Our project started its scriptwriting and storyboarding phase in February of 2026, and completed production in early April of 2026.
